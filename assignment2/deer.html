Cool Types of Deer

Deer can live in many different environments. Some types are known for their antlers, size, or ability to live in extreme climates.

Muntjac

These deer are also known as barking deer because of the sounds they make.

Reindeer

Both male and female reindeer can grow antlers.

Pudu

The pudu is one of the smallest deer species in the world and lives in South America.
