Our Story

We specialize in used vinyls and rare collections

Melody Records is a curated record store dedicated to finding unique vinyls for music enthusiasts. Our store carries albums from rock, alternative, jazz, pop, and indie genres. We believe that records are small pieces of history and more than just music.

Visitors can explore our current inventory in person at our store or online to discorer new artists and find rare vinyls. Our staff are always on the hunt for new additions to our collection, so visitors can also request an album for us to search for.
