This selection of books is a collection of antique works, dating back to 1895. Many of the books included are old textbooks (schoolbooks) that were used in middleschools
and highschools.
Some titles include:

  • Instructive lessons for the young
  • The first reader
  • Analysis of civil government
  • The principles of the American government
  • The fashionable American letter writer

The textbooks that are included in this collection are mostly history books with faded, burnt oragnge colored covers. Additionally, the covers of these editions are hardbacks.
However, there are two paperback textbooks. These two textbooks have some light wear on the covers and various markings on the pages.

The most expensive book that is included was published in 1942 and has been signed by the author. His signature has been authenticated by reputable local antique dealers.
