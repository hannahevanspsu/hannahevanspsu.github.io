Understanding Skin Type

Everyone's skin is different, so learning your skin type is the best first step to choosing the products that work best for you.

Dry Skin

Dry skin usually feels tight, rough, or flaky because it lacks moisture. Rich moisturizers and hydrating ingredients are recommended.

Oily Skin

Oily skin produces excess sebum and can appear shiny. Lightweight moisturizers and gentle cleansers are ususally the most helpful.

Combination Skin

Combination skin has both oily and dry areas. The T-zone is usually oily while the cheeks are dry.

Normal Skin

Normal skin is generally balanced with few blemishes, not a lot of sensitivity, and medium hydration.

Sensitive Skin

Sensitive skin is easily irritated by products or the environment. Fragrance free products are best.

General Skincare Tips

  • Wash your face with a gentle cleanser twice daily.
  • Wear sunscreen every day, even on the cloudy ones.
  • Drink lots of water to support cellular turnover and skin health.
  • Avoid using harsh products too much.
  • Moisturize regularly to maintain your skin barrier.
