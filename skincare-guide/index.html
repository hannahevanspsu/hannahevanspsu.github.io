Welcome to The Glow Guide

Taking care of your skin doesn't have to be confusing. Whether you have dry, oily, combination, normal, or sensitive skin, understanding your skin's needs is the first step to building a skincare routine that works. This website introduces common skin types, features popular skincare ingredients, and provides simple, basic routines to help you achieve your healthy skin goals.

Cleanse

Cleansing removes dirt, oil, sunscreen, bacteria, and makeup while prepping your skin for the rest of your routine.

Moisturize

Moisturizers help strengthen the skin barrier and keep your skin hydrated.

Protect

Daily sunscreen helps protect your skin from harmful UV rays and reduces premature aging.
