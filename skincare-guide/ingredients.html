Common Skincare Ingredients

Understanding ingredients helps you choose products that can target your concerns.

Hyaluronic Acid

Hyaluronic acid attracts water to the skin, making it an ingredient for hydration and maintaining a healthy skin barrier.

Niacinamide

Niacinamide helps reduce redness, minimize the appearance of pores, and strengthen the skin barrier.

Vitamin C

Vitamin C brightens the complexion, helps fade dark spots, and provides antioxidant protection from environmental damage.

Retinol

Retinol encourages cell turnover, improves the appearance of fine lines, wrinkles, and acne.

Salicylic Acid

Salicylic acid dissolves excess oil and dead skin cells from pores, making it ideal for acne prone skin.

Tips for Choosing Skincare Products

  • Read ingredient labels before purchasing.
  • Introduce one new product at a time, avoid overloading.
  • Patch test products before full use.
  • Wear sunscreen when using active ingredients.
  • Be patient as results usually take several weeks.
  • Consult a dermatologist for continuing concerns.
